## liminalia

`609c368` — hatred has its own ledger.

Every crime so far came from desperation: pressure from debt, unemployment, hunger, poverty. Murder comes from the other direction. The relationship graph already tracked rivals (affinity −0.6 or worse), but rivalry was as cosmetic as partnership was before households.

### the hatred ledger

Rivals forced to share a node accumulate hatred per hour — 6/h, deterred by policing like any other pressure. A watched citizen swallows it. Past its own threshold the citizen kills the rival where they stand.

### consequences

The victim dies with cause Murder and their estate settles through the shared death machinery — home and workplace released, money to the household or the treasury. The murderer gets a crime record and an officer dispatched like any other criminal. If caught: a 100 fine in money or debt and 72 hours in jail, three times the ordinary sentence. There is no stolen money to return.

Six new tests, 334 total.
