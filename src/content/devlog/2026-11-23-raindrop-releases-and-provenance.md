Raindrop moved from a foundation to something you can hand to someone: tagged releases, record views, and a visual identity that says where a document came from.

- Verified release records for v0.1.0 through v0.11.0.
- Records listed for collection `at://` URIs, with pagination for collection listings.
- Post richtext facets rendered safely.
- `at://` document customization and blob/authenticity policies.
- Raindrop theme and an `at://` provenance header, with the favicon on `at://` pages.

Provenance in the header is a small detail with a large effect. A document in a browser should say where it came from before you have to ask.
