The two console clients moved in lockstep this week: the same missing social features, then the same performance work.

- Private bookmarks and resumable drafts on both clients.
- Reports for posts and accounts, starter-pack browsing, and list creation and membership management.
- Profile display name and bio editing, and muting a conversation from the thread screen.
- Feed discovery and management infrastructure in the shared layer, with the UI on Cobalt's Feeds screen and Indigo's search screen.
- UI pass: cached text measurements, a benchmark that times layout apart from the host blitter, and long-label fitting.
- Releases at 0.16.0 and 0.17.0 on each client; Cobalt later folded in a group-DM display fix at 0.17.0.

Parity between a Wii U app and a 3DS app is easier to keep when the feature list is written once and implemented twice in the same week.
