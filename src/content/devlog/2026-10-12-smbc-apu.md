SMBC makes sound (#35). `hw/apu` now keeps a per-frame write log so the synth sees mid-frame register traffic, and $4015 reads report channel status instead of the last written value. `hw/apu_synth.c` models the four channels the game uses: two pulses with duty, envelope, sweep and length counters; triangle with its linear counter; noise with both feedback modes. The 4-step frame sequencer free-runs at NTSC frame boundaries, and the non-linear NESdev mixer combines the channels.

Synthesis is cycle-stepped at the CPU clock (1789773 Hz) and resampled to 44.1 kHz. `host/audio.c` is now a lock-free SPSC ring on C11 atomics: the frontend pushes each frame's samples after `smbc_frame`, the SDL callback drains. Engine-driven smoke: starting a game and running 1300 frames yields 1291 audible frames of the overworld theme at a peak of 0.51 — no clipping. Twelve white-box test groups cover envelope decay and loop, length halt and clocking, sweep, the linear counter, noise modes and the mixer.

One test-data bug worth noting: 0x9f is 10011111b — d5 *clear* — so the halt-flag probe needed 0xbf. Reading hex bitfields by eye remains a reliable way to lie to yourself.
