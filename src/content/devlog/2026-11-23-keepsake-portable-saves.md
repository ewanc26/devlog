Keepsake's premise was always that identity leaves a mark on the world. It now has save formats that can survive that claim.

- Versioned save records with migration for older saves.
- Portable character bundles.
- Versioned, documented DID-derived world seeds.
- DID-seeded variance, with tests for the previously untested seams.
- Narrative content: the letter and locket of names, your mark on the gate, and The Quiet Bell side quest with UI tests.
- Version 0.4.0, recorded as the first tagged release.

Deterministic seeds tied to an identity only matter if the save can move. The bundle and migration work is what makes the premise portable rather than a neat trick in one directory.
