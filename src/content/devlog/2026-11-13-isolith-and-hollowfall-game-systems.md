Both game repos spent the week adding ways to move through a level, then made sure players could understand what they were seeing.

### isolith

- Dash, impact shake and particle bursts, with The Abyss and Zenith courses.
- Ice as a low-friction surface, with the Frostline course and 19 smoke checks.
- Conveyor belts as a flow surface, with the Slipstream course and 34 smoke checks.
- Sideways wind walls, rising wind columns and a Crosswinds course using both.
- A how-to-play reference screen, a camera-shake setting, a CHANGELOG and version 0.2.0.

### Hollowfall

- The first four pacts, then the Twin Gate on a PortalSystem of listed openings, then Open Hand to complete the designed five.
- Portal-aware sound and a non-colour cue for the pair.
- Full Gàidhlig interface support, Gaeilge beside it, and a note on what still appears in English.

Systems, courses and translations in the same week is the right order: build the thing, then make it legible.
